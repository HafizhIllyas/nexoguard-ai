A: "NexoGuard is a Decision Support System, not an autonomous agent. We enforce deterministic FIGO safety guardrails: even if the ML confidence drops, critical physiological markers trigger hard-coded fail-safe alerts. Additionally, our SHAP cards provide full transparency so clinicians audit the reasoning behind every alert."

Q: "Why didn't you use a large Transformer or Deep Neural Network?"

A: "NexoPrima already tried a deep dense network and showed that complex black-box networks suffer from low precision on small clinical cohorts. Our Physiology-Informed Hybrid model achieves 96% sensitivity, runs in under 20ms on basic hospital edge CPUs, and complies with international FIGO standards."
